How to Use This Site

This is our league tracker for the {{year}} ATP season. Use the tabs above to make picks, view standings, and explore predictions.

Order of Exploring

Add your name on the Standings page in order to add your picks. Check out the Predict page to see who are the likely winners for each event and maybe check H2H. Make your predictions on the Enter Picks page and then check out your picks on the Breakdown page. Keep coming back to the website to see where you rank among your competitors!

Tabs Overview

Standings

Shows the leaderboard for the season (total points). Add people here if they don’t show up yet.

Breakdown

Shows each event and everyone’s pick, plus the result/points once entered. Use this to see who picked what and where points came from.

Enter Picks

Pick one player per event. Submitting again overwrites your previous pick for that event. Use the dropdown suggestions when typing a player name to avoid spelling issues.

Model

Predict:
  • Tournament Odds: gives win probabilities for top players (no draw bracket needed).
  • Head-to-head: predicts a match winner using Elo + head-to-head + optional tournament history.

Draw Simulator:
  • Enter the draws for any ATP tournaments and see which players should come out on top!

Model Picks:
  • A trained ML model has figured out the parameters to guess the winners for the main events. Check to see who they predicted after the draws have been released!

Admin

Only the commissioner should use these:
  • Model: runs the data update + rebuilds Elo/H2H/event records.
  • Enter Results: enters the round reached for picked players (used to compute points).
  • Bracket Post: Commissioner runs the model to predict the winner.

How Scoring Works

Points are awarded based on the round your picked player reached for each event. The commissioner enters results, and your total updates automatically.

Tips

  • Use autocomplete suggestions when typing player names.
  • If a player name is rejected, it means it isn’t in the database spelling — pick from suggestions.
  • Standings updates after results are entered.
