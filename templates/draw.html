🏆 Draw Simulator — How to Use

Use this simulator to explore win probabilities and likely paths for a tennis tournament draw.
This tool does not save results or affect league standings.
📝 Tournament name
A label for your reference only (e.g. Australian Open 2026). This does not affect predictions and is not saved publicly.
🎾 Surface
Court surface the tournament is played on (Hard, Clay, Grass, etc.). Players have surface-specific strengths, so this meaningfully affects outcomes.
🏟️ Level
Tournament importance:
  • G – Grand Slam
  • M – Masters 1000
  • 500 / 250 – ATP Tour events
Higher levels slightly favor elite players in close matches.
🧮 Best of
Number of sets required to win a match.
Best-of-5 reduces randomness and favors stronger players.
🔁 Simulations (Sims)
Number of times the tournament is simulated.
  • 1,000–2,000: fast and stable
  • 5,000+: smoother probabilities (slower)
🎲 Seed (advanced)
Controls randomness for reproducibility.
You almost never need to change this — the default is recommended.
📋 Draw entries
Paste the draw one player per line, in bracket order.
  • Total lines must be 32 / 64 / 128
  • Each match uses two consecutive lines
  • BYE, Q, LL, WC are allowed
📊 Output
After simulation you’ll see:
  • Champion win probabilities
  • Most likely winner of each match
  • Per-round advancement odds
⚠️ Results are probabilistic, not guarantees.
